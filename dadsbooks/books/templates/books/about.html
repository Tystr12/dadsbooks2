About this site

Gråskjegg Bøker (and its sibling shelf, Gråskjegg Musikk) is a way to showcase a personal collection of books, vinyl, and CDs. Browse what's here, and if something catches your eye, get in touch through the item's own page — or the contact page.

It's also a learning project: a chance to build and actually run a real site, from the catalog itself to deploying it for real, rather than just another tutorial exercise. Thanks for stopping by.
